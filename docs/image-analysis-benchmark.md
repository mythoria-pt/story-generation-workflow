# Image analysis resolution benchmark

Measured on 23 November 2026 using Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the existing image-analysis prompt and JSON schema, high thinking, and high media resolution. Each fixture was analysed twice at its original size and twice with its longest edge reduced to 768 px. The runs alternated variant order. Calls used the application provider directly, including its token-count request. The benchmark used in-memory buffers and made no storage or database writes.

| Fixture                   | Full to scaled dimensions | Full to scaled bytes | Median wall time, full to scaled | Quality                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Workshop photo            | 1024×1536 to 512×768      | 396,267 to 117,952   | 22.37 s to 25.59 s               | Both variants classified it as a photo and detected five people in both runs. Approximate box overlap against the same reference changed by at most 0.01 per person.     |
| Synthetic drawing         | 2800×1800 to 768×494      | 241,085 to 74,440    | 4.99 s to 7.43 s                 | Both variants classified it as a drawing and detected both cats. Reference box overlap for the two cats was 0.83 and 0.78 at full size and 0.82–0.83 and 0.78 at 768 px. |
| Synthetic fine-text sheet | 3200×2400 to 768×576      | 279,409 to 56,987    | 5.88 s to 6.58 s                 | Both variants classified it as text. The original recovered all eight registry codes in both runs. The resize recovered seven in both runs.                              |

Prompt token counts were identical within each fixture across sizes, at 2,547 for the photo, 2,442 for the drawing and 2,440 for the text. All 12 responses completed successfully. These are local wall times with a small sample. They exclude the app upload, Cloud Storage and metadata persistence. The supplied photo is the public Mythoria workshop hero asset `workshop-paper-to-book-iphone.jpg`. The synthetic drawing used two cats separated horizontally. The text sheet printed eight known five-character codes beside registry labels at 48, 42, 36, 32, 27, 22, 18 and 14 px.

Keep original-resolution inputs for analysis. The smaller payload showed no consistent speed or token benefit in this run and lost fine OCR information. Use the existing image-analysis stage timing logs to distinguish download, model inference and persistence when comparing future changes.
